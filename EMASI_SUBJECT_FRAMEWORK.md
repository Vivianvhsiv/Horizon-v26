# Horizon v24 — EMASI Subject Framework

Horizon currently uses 13 EMASI subject inputs recorded for this project:

1. Mathematics — Toán
2. Literature — Văn
3. English — Tiếng Anh
4. Physics — Lý
5. Biology — Sinh
6. Chemistry — Hoá học
7. Music — Âm nhạc
8. Fine Arts — Mỹ thuật
9. ICT
10. Economics and Law — Kinh tế pháp luật
11. Technology — Công nghệ
12. Geography — Địa lý (S12)
13. History — Lịch sử (S13)

The subject inventory reflects the project owner's requested scope. These subjects are exploration inputs. They are **not** a claim that every major requires all 13 subjects, and they are **not** universal university admission prerequisites.

## Evidence gate
A subject-to-major edge is displayed in the network only when its record has status `Verified` or `Supported` and includes institution-specific curriculum evidence, an explanation, and a traceable source. `Pending Verification` records must not create network edges.

## Relationship categories
- Direct Academic Connection
- Related Knowledge Connection
- No Direct Connection Identified

Absence of a direct connection is not evidence that a student is unable or unsuitable to study a major.

## Current data status
The 20 major profiles and their university source records are retained from earlier releases. They are not upgraded to fully verified by this release. Geography and History guidance is exploratory and pathway-dependent. The actual EMASI syllabus and grade-level learning outcomes are still needed before claims of precise curriculum alignment can be made.

See `data/emasi_subjects.json`, `data/emasi_mapping_protocol.json`, `data/majors.json`, and `data/academic_sources.json`.
