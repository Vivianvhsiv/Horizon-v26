# Horizon Academic Evidence Standard — v14

## Purpose
This standard makes the basis, scope and verification status of academic claims visible. A prestigious source does not automatically verify every claim on a page; each claim must be checked against the cited material.

## Evidence categories
- **Disciplinary evidence:** supports a relationship between a major and an academic discipline.
- **Curriculum evidence:** supports courses or requirements at a named institution and degree pathway only.
- **Professional education guidance:** supports broad learning goals or standards published by a professional association.
- **Career evidence:** supports occupational descriptions or pathways. This requires a separate official occupational source audit.

## Verification states
- **Verified:** the cited source directly supports the specific claim shown.
- **Partially verified:** some claims are supported, while other parts of the profile remain unreviewed.
- **Pending:** claim-level sources have not yet been checked.
- **Conflicting:** reliable sources disagree and the difference needs explanation.

## Required source record fields
- claim
- relationship_type / evidence_category
- institution
- source_title
- source_url
- source_type
- date_accessed
- scope
- verification_status
- reviewer_note

## Rules
1. Never use a general homepage to support a specific course or relationship if a direct program/catalog page exists.
2. Distinguish general disciplinary relationships from institution-specific curriculum requirements.
3. Do not infer admission prerequisites from related subjects or typical coursework.
4. Do not infer student suitability from evidence strength.
5. Record source conflicts and limitations rather than silently choosing one.
6. Recheck sources before public release; pages and curricula can change.
