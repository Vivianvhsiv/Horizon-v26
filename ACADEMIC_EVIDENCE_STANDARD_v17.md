# Horizon Academic Evidence Standard — v17

## Scope
- Geographic scope: Vietnam, United States, and other international universities. Existing official programme-source coverage is retained from v16.
- Initial product scope: undergraduate majors/programmes.
- School-subject scope: the 13 current EMASI subjects listed in `data/emasi_subjects.json`.

## Major profile requirements
Each major profile should describe its overview, academic foundations, areas of study, related disciplines, specialized applications, research areas, skills, possible career areas, limitations, university programmes, and references. University-specific curricula and admission prerequisites must be distinguished.

## Connection requirements
A confirmed EMASI subject-to-major relationship must contain: subject ID, major ID, relationship type, specific EMASI curriculum evidence, named university and programme, named university course or learning outcome, explanation, source URL, source section/page, verification status, and last-reviewed date.

## Evidence statuses
- `Verified`: direct, specific primary-source evidence has been checked.
- `Supported`: reliable academic evidence supports a related-knowledge connection, but not a universal requirement.
- `Pending Verification`: not displayed as a confirmed network edge.
- `Rejected / Not Supported`: reviewed evidence does not support the proposed relationship.

## Source interpretation
A university programme directory establishes programme availability only. It does not, by itself, establish curriculum content, subject relationships, admission requirements, skills, or career outcomes. Each claim needs the source type appropriate to that claim. One university's curriculum must not be generalized to all universities.

## Non-inference safeguards
Horizon is an exploration and reflection tool. It does not determine aptitude, predict admission, rank students, or infer that a student is unsuitable because a school subject is not connected to a major.

## v18 — EMASI subject-choice guidance (bilingual)
Every sample major now has bilingual guidance mapping relevant EMASI subjects into three practical categories: Strong preparation, Helpful support, and Pathway-dependent (plus Application-dependent where relevant). This is exploratory guidance synthesized from each major's broad academic foundations and its cited discipline/programme references. It is not a verified institution-specific prerequisite map. Programme-specific subject relationships remain unconfirmed until the relevant EMASI curriculum content is compared with a named university programme's official course descriptions or admissions requirements.

For interdisciplinary or broad majors, a bilingual pathway note explains how subject choices can differ by subfield. Students are advised to begin with the strongest broad foundations, then choose supporting subjects according to the academic pathway they wish to explore. No subject combination is treated as universal.
