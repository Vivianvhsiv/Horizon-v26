# Horizon v7 — Academic Foundation Audit

## Scope
Reviewed all 20 major profiles in the v6 `data/majors.json`. Added a consistent academic taxonomy while retaining legacy fields so the existing Streamlit interface remains compatible.

## Important interpretation
- **Academic foundations**: core knowledge areas commonly used to define or teach the field.
- **Related disciplines**: neighboring disciplines or methods that contribute to some tracks.
- **Specialized applications**: narrower research or professional applications, not universal foundations.
- **Useful school subjects**: possible preparation; never presented as universal admission prerequisites.
- **Research areas**: topics students may explore; not guaranteed courses at every institution.
- **Limitations**: cautions on program variation, professional licensing, and scope.

## Key corrections
- Cognitive Science no longer treats Music, Art & Design or Media Studies as general core school subjects. Music cognition, visual cognition and design cognition remain specialized research applications.
- Psychology is represented as a scientific discipline with research methods and statistics, not only a helping profession. Clinical and other regulated pathways carry a graduate-training/credential caveat.
- Across all majors, disciplinary foundations are separated from high-school preparation subjects and specialized applications.
- The legacy `subjects` key is retained for interface compatibility, with notes directing future UI work to `useful_school_subjects`.

## Evidence status and limitations
This is an academic taxonomy audit, not a complete validation of every course in every university. Source URLs identify reputable professional or institutional reference points. They do not prove that every listed subfield is required in every degree. The data intentionally retains a pending institution-specific verification status. Before public launch, each major should be checked against at least two current university curricula in the intended countries, and dated verification metadata should be added.

## Technical compatibility
- `app.py`, `requirements.txt`, branding assets and the existing app flow are preserved from v6.
- Existing fields (`subjects`, `disciplines`, `interests`, `careers`, etc.) remain present.
