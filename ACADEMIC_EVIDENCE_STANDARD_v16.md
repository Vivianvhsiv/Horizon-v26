# Horizon Academic Evidence Standard — v16

## What changed
- Expanded the official university catalogue coverage across all 20 Horizon majors.
- Added a separate **Programme availability evidence** claim for additional universities.
- Recorded the exact programme label shown on the university page when it differs from Horizon’s major label.
- Kept programme availability separate from disciplinary, curriculum, school-subject, skills, research, and career claims.

## Interpretation rules
- A university catalogue listing verifies only that the named institution publishes a programme with that title (or the exact related title recorded).
- It does not prove that every institution offers the same degree level, curriculum, prerequisites, or professional pathway.
- Related titles (for example, Applied Linguistics, Biological Sciences, or Media and Communication Studies) are explicitly labelled and should not be silently treated as identical degrees.
- The overall major evidence status remains conservative. Additional catalogue coverage does not automatically upgrade the full major to Verified.
- URLs are official university programme/catalogue pages and should be rechecked before public release because offerings can change.

## Limitations
This version broadens institutional coverage, but it is not a complete worldwide census of every university offering each major. It prioritizes official programme lists that could be checked in this update. A future claim-level audit should add department curriculum pages and direct sources for each academic relationship, school-subject suggestion, skill, research area, and career pathway.
