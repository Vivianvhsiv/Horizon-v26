# Horizon source URL manual review — 24 November 2026

## Scope

This review follows the automated live URL audit of 86 unique URLs, which marked 10 URLs for manual review because of access restrictions, transient responses, or unclear HTTP behavior. The checks below inspect the official page content surfaced by web retrieval. A successful search/index result is treated as content inspection, not a guarantee that every visitor can load the page directly.

## Important correction

The VGU PDF is an undergraduate application guideline, not a course curriculum. It must not be cited as evidence for the content of Mechanical Engineering or Electrical Engineering curricula. Its scope is limited to programme names and admission information actually stated in the document. For curriculum claims, use a current official programme curriculum page or catalog instead.

## Remaining limitations

- The automated status “REVIEW” is a conservative transport/access signal. It does not mean the source is invalid.
- Conversely, search indexing or a successful HTTP response does not prove that a source supports every claim attached to it.
- These ten URLs have now been manually inspected at the page-content level, but the full 65-claim register still requires a separate claim-by-claim semantic review.
- None of these sources establishes equivalence with EMASI's current high-school syllabus or a universal admissions prerequisite.
- The actual EMASI syllabi / learning outcomes remain necessary to validate individual subject suggestions.
